AxleLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

AxleLog is an IFTA fuel tax and mileage app made by Crafted Chaos Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it goes, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, email us at craftedchaoslabs@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect what is needed to keep your mileage and fuel log, sync it, and calculate your IFTA fuel tax.
  • GPS location is recorded only while a trip that you started is running. Your detailed route stays on your phone.
  • When you choose to scan a receipt, the photo is sent to Anthropic's AI to read it. Anthropic does not use it to train its models.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app.

1. Your account

When you create an account we collect:

  • Email and password. Your password is handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, which stores only a scrambled (hashed) version. We never see it.
  • Your name, if you enter one.
  • Google or Apple sign-in. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them (Apple may give us a private relay address instead). For Apple sign-in we also store a token from Apple so that we can tell Apple to disconnect AxleLog if you delete your account.
  • Your business or fleet: its name, your role (owner, manager or driver), and invite codes that owners and managers create.

We send you emails with verification and password reset codes. We do not send marketing email.

2. Your log

The app stores what you enter:

  • Entries: date, state or province, miles or kilometers, fuel quantity, fuel type, cost, price per unit, currency, station or vendor name, tolls, repairs, washes, notes, truck number, and whether fuel was for a reefer or had tax paid at the pump.
  • Trucks and trailers: unit number, nickname, make, model, year, VIN, license plate and plate state, fuel type.
  • Tax rates you enter by hand to override the published IFTA rates.

3. Location and GPS trips

  • Only during a trip you start. AxleLog records your location only while a GPS trip you started is running. Recording stops when you end the trip. You never have to use GPS: you can enter miles by hand.
  • Background location. If you allow location "Always", the app keeps recording while it is in the background or the screen is off, but only while a trip is running. On Android a notification shows the whole time. On iPhone, during a trip, the app also sets a geofence about 1 km (0.6 mi) around the truck's last position so iPhone can restart recording if the app was closed. It is removed when the trip ends.
  • Worked out on your phone. The app works out which state or province you are in on your phone, using boundary maps built into the app. Your coordinates are not sent to us or to any location service for this.
  • Your route stays on your phone. The detailed route (the GPS points) is never uploaded. You can delete stored routes at any time in Settings › Privacy; deleting the app deletes them too.
  • What is synced: a trip summary (start and end time, start and end state or province, odometer readings you enter, GPS miles, the number and length of gaps in recording, the tracking mode, notes, and the truck and trailer) and the miles driven in each state or province per day.
  • Filling in the state. When you add an entry or scan a receipt, and you have already allowed location access, the app may use your current trip or the phone's last known location to suggest the state or province. This happens on your phone and never asks for permission by itself.
  • Maps. On iPhone, trip routes are shown on Apple Maps, which loads map images from Apple for the area on screen. On Android the app draws the route itself without a map service.

4. Receipt photos and AI receipt scanning

  • Photos you add are saved in the app on your phone. When your log syncs, they are uploaded to private cloud storage so they are backed up and available on your other devices (and to your fleet's owner and managers, see section 5).
  • Scanning is optional. When you tap Scan, a smaller copy of the photo (about 1600 pixels on the longest side) is sent over an encrypted connection, through our server, to Anthropic's Claude API. Claude reads the printed details (such as date, station, location, quantity, price, total and fuel type) and sends them back for you to check before you save. Photos you don't scan are never sent to Anthropic.
  • What our server keeps. Our server does not save the photo or what Claude read from it. It records that a scan happened (when, and whether it worked) to apply a daily scan limit.
  • What Anthropic does with it. Anthropic's Commercial Terms state that "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services," and its privacy center says that by default it does not use inputs or outputs from its API to train its models. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, unless it needs to keep them longer to enforce its Usage Policy or to comply with the law.

5. Fleets: who can see your data

  • Solo owner-operators: only you can see your log.
  • In a fleet: the fleet's owner and managers can see every member's entries, trip summaries, receipt photos, trucks and trailers, and the members' names, roles and join dates. Drivers see only their own log. Nobody in the fleet can see your email address or your detailed GPS routes.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or show ads. The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

7. Diagnostics and logs

  • The app does not collect crash reports or usage analytics itself. If you have allowed it in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may share crash reports with us. They don't include your log.
  • Our providers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, time and the kind of request) for a limited time for security and troubleshooting. Our server functions log errors, which may include your account ID but never your receipt photos or their contents.

8. How we use your data

We use your data only to:

  • run the app: keep your log, sync it between devices, calculate IFTA, build reports and exports, and read receipts you scan;
  • keep accounts secure, send sign-in codes, and prevent abuse (such as the daily scan limit);
  • answer your emails.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not use it to train AI models.

9. Feedback and exports

  • Send Feedback opens your email app with the app version, build number, platform and OS version filled in. You see everything before you send it. When you email us we receive your email address and what you write.
  • Exports and backups you make are created on your phone. Where you send them is up to you.

10. How long we keep data

  • Your account and log: as long as you have an account.
  • Things you delete (entries, trips, trucks, trailers) disappear from the app right away. In our database they are marked as deleted, so the deletion reaches your other devices, and they are removed for good when your account, or for owners the fleet, is deleted.
  • Scan records (time and result): until your account is deleted.
  • Photos sent to Anthropic: up to 30 days at Anthropic, as described in section 4.
  • Technical logs: for the limited time our providers keep them.

11. Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app: Settings › Account › Delete My Account. You can also email us and we will do it for you.

  • Owners, including solo owner-operators: your business or fleet is deleted with all of its entries, trip summaries, tax rates, invite codes and receipt photos, along with your trucks, trailers, scan records and sign-in. You type the fleet name to confirm.
  • Drivers and managers in someone else's fleet: your sign-in, name, trucks, trailers and scan records are deleted. Entries, trip summaries and receipt photos you added stay with the fleet, because they are part of the fleet's tax records, but they are no longer linked to you. To have them removed, ask the fleet owner or email us.
  • Apple sign-in: we tell Apple to disconnect AxleLog.

Data on your phone. Signing out or deleting your account removes your account's data from that phone. Entries made before you signed in, which were never part of an account, stay on the phone until you delete them or the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the phone. We cannot delete exports you have saved or sent elsewhere.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Location: you can refuse location access, allow it only while using the app, or turn it off at any time in your phone's settings. You can always enter miles by hand.
  • Receipt scanning is optional.
  • Access and correction: you can see and edit your data in the app and export it. You can also email us to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion. We will reply within 30 days.
  • California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, to have it corrected or deleted, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information.
  • Canadian residents have the right to access and correct their personal information and to withdraw consent. Your data is stored and processed in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under US law. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.

Crafted Chaos Labs is responsible for your personal information. Contact craftedchaoslabs@gmail.com for any privacy question or request.

13. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).
  • Server-side rules make sure each person can reach only their own data, and owners and managers only their own fleet's data.
  • Receipt photos are kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links.
  • The log stored in the app on your phone is protected by your phone's own lock and encryption, so use a screen lock.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires.

14. Children

AxleLog is for commercial drivers and businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we will also tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
