3. IFTA tax rates

  • AxleLog uses the quarterly tax rate charts published by IFTA, Inc. (iftach.org). Our server checks for new charts once a day, so a new or corrected chart can take a day or more to reach the app.
  • Until a quarter's chart is published, AxleLog estimates with the most recent published rates and marks the estimate as provisional.
  • Rates you enter by hand override the published ones in your account. You are responsible for those.
  • We do not guarantee that the rates in AxleLog are complete, current or free of errors. The official charts from IFTA, Inc. and your base jurisdiction take precedence.

4. Receipt scanning can make mistakes

Receipt scanning uses artificial intelligence (Anthropic's Claude) to read photos of receipts. It can misread or miss details, especially on faded, crumpled or unusual receipts. Always check what it fills in before you save an entry. You are responsible for the entries you save.

5. GPS and odometer accuracy

GPS miles are worked out from your phone's location, which can be inaccurate or interrupted (for example by poor signal, tunnels, battery saving, the app being closed, or location access being turned off). AxleLog shows gaps when it knows about them, but it can't guarantee that every mile is recorded in the right state or province. Odometer readings are only as accurate as what you enter. Check your trips before relying on them for a return.

Trip detection ("Ask me" or "Automatic") is a convenience. It can miss a drive, start a trip late, or end one during a long stop, and it can mistake a drive that isn't for work (for example as a passenger) for one that is. Check each detected trip, and confirm only trips that belong in your records.
